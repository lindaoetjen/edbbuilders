Getting started

What does EDB Builders build?+

ADUs, custom homes, remodels and large-scale remodels, additions, garage conversions and commercial construction. We also do concrete flatwork and foundations, swimming pools, spas, water features, hardscape, landscaping and masonry.

Where do you work?+

The greater part of Northern California. Our office is at 271 Opportunity St, Suite C, Sacramento, CA 95838.

How do I start a project?+

Call or text (916) 360-0445, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm, or send the form on our Contact page with a few lines about your lot and what you want to build.

Is there a free evaluation?+

Yes. Concrete and foundation work starts with a free on-site evaluation.

Money and financing

Do you help with financing?+

Yes. We offer an array of financing options tailored to you, and a financial assessment to find the best path forward. Cost, financing options and return on investment are the first step of every project.

What does an ADU cost?+

Typical California ranges from our ADU guide: garage conversion $100k to $250k, attached ADU $200k to $400k+, detached ADU $250k to $600k+. Your lot, size and finishes set the real number.

How do payments work?+

After permits are issued, we walk through final costs, confirm your budget and set a clear, step-by-step payment schedule, so everything stays transparent and predictable.

How much contingency should I plan for a custom home?+

Our custom home guide recommends keeping a contingency of 10 to 15% of the budget.

ADUs

What kinds of ADU do you build?+

Detached ADUs, attached ADUs and garage conversions.

How many ADU models do you offer?+

Ten pre-selected models, from 439 to 1,200 sq ft. If none fits, we can give you access to over 200 additional designs or design a complete custom solution.

Can I build an ADU on my lot?+

According to our ADU guide, ADUs are allowed on most residential lots in California, detached ADUs are typically up to 1,200 sq ft, parking is often waived, and cities decide within 60 days of a complete application. We confirm the rules for your lot.

Why build an ADU?+

Multigenerational living, a private workspace, or an investment that earns rental income and can add property value.

Design and permits

Do you design the project?+

Yes. Our design team works closely with you throughout, whether you choose a model, one of 200+ designs or a fully custom design.

Do you handle the permits?+

Yes. Once your design is finalized we prepare a complete permit plan set and handle the entire permitting process, from application to final approval.

Which finishes can I choose?+

Any model can take different finishes, for example Modern, Tuscan, Contemporary or Farmhouse, chosen with our design team during the style and finish selection period. Appliances come in a small or large GE package.

Can I split my lot or sell part of it?+

California lot laws now let homeowners separate or sell part of a lot. Our guide covers the five steps: check local rules, survey, documents, apply, finalize.

During the build

How will I know how the build is going?+

Staff follow your project daily. Each week you get a log with progress notes, schedule and timeline evaluation, and progress photos. You can log in to your client portal any time, and our open-book model lets you follow along even from afar.

How do you check quality?+

Quality control runs through every build, with daily and weekly site inspections.

What happens at the end?+

Our team does an internal walkthrough first. Then you do your completion walkthrough, point out anything that needs attention, we address the final items together, and we hand over the keys.

Are you licensed?+

Yes. EDB Builders and Consulting holds California contractor license CSLB #1145705, classes B (general building) and C-53 (swimming pool).

Pools and concrete

Which pool work do you do?+

Swimming pools, spas and water features, plus the pool decks, patios and hardscape around them.

Which pool deck finishes are there?+

Broom finish, stamped concrete and cool-deck.

What patios and driveways do you build?+

Patios in stamped, stained or polished concrete, with pergolas, outdoor kitchens and fire pits. Driveways in broom, exposed aggregate, stamped or coloured finishes, plus sidewalks and pathways.

Which foundations do you pour?+

Slab-on-grade, raised stem wall, pier and beam and post-tension foundations, plus slabs for garages, sheds, workshops and ADUs, with soil reports, seismic design, drainage and control joints.
