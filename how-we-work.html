How we work

Finance first.Build smart.

Four steps and one team, from the first budget talk to the day you get the keys.

The four steps

Every project follows the same four steps, from the first budget talk to the day you get the keys.

Plan and finance

Cost, financing options and your return on investment, worked out before anything is designed.

Design and permits

A model, one of 200+ designs or a custom design, then a complete permit plan set and the permitting from application to approval.

Build and quality

Quality control, daily and weekly site inspections, and a client portal to follow the build.

Completion

Our internal walkthrough first, then your completion walkthrough, then the keys.

Fully managed

Open book,every day.

Staff follow your project daily. Each week you get a log with progress notes, schedule and timeline, and progress photos. Log in to your portal any time; with our open-book model you can follow even from afar.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you help with financing?+

Yes. We offer an array of financing options tailored to you, with a financial assessment of the best path forward.

Do you handle the permits?+

Yes. We prepare a complete permit plan set and handle the entire permitting process, from application to final approval.

What if none of the ADU models fit?+

We can design a complete custom solution, or give you access to over 200 additional designs.

How will I know how it is going?+

Staff follow the project daily. You get weekly logs with progress notes, schedule and photos, and a client portal you can log in to any time.

How do payments work?+

After permits are issued, we walk through final costs, confirm your budget and set a clear step-by-step payment schedule.

Which areas do you work in?+

The greater part of Northern California. Our office is in Sacramento.
